# Arena

A persistent gladiator arena: a fixed roster fights a duel every hour, and players stake virtual points on the next fight.

## Language

**Bet position**:
What a player can do on the announced fight: the bet they have placed, the stakes open to them on each corner, or nothing because betting has closed.
_Avoid_: bet options, bet state

**Stake options**:
The fixed set of stakes a player may choose from. Any other amount is not a stake.
_Avoid_: stake limits, min stake, max stake
